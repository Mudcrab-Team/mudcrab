# AI-assisted contributions

> **Proposal** (#78): agreed by a contributor, waiting for a maintainer's confirmation. Follow it for now and comment on the issue if you disagree.

AI tools are welcome here. These rules keep their help useful without swamping review.

1. **Disclose.** A PR, issue or comment written with an AI assistant says so in one line at the top, for example `> Written with an AI assistant, checked by @you.` Commits made with an assistant carry a trailer naming it: `Assisted-by: <tool>` or `Co-authored-by: <assistant> <email>`. With a squash merge, the maintainer copies those trailers into the merge description; the PR body only needs the one-line note.
2. **A human owns every line.** The contributor reads, tests and answers for everything they submit, and answers reviewers themselves or approves each reply.
3. **No autonomous posting to people.** An agent never opens PRs or issues, comments, or pushes without its human's OK for that item. Replies to bots, such as CodeRabbit, are fine when labelled as AI-written.
4. **Don't flood review.** Keep about five PRs open per contributor, and split large work into PRs that each stand on their own.
5. **Check and announce first.** Search issues and PRs before starting, and comment before building in an area someone else has claimed ([workflow.md](contributing/workflow.md)).
