# Skyrim Forward-Shades, Then Grades in One Pass

Vanilla Skyrim SE/AE lights the world with a **forward renderer that has one deferred trick**. Every opaque `BSLightingShader` draw evaluates the sun, a 3x4 "directional ambient" matrix (DALC) and **up to 7 point lights, at most 4 of them shadowed**. The light counts are baked into the shader permutation key. Shadows are resolved beforehand into a screen-space RGBA shadow mask, and the forward shader samples it by pixel position. The lighting math is non-PBR and runs in gamma space: Lambert diffuse, unnormalized Blinn-Phong specular, a `1 - (d/r)^2` falloff that ignores Gamebryo's attenuation fields, and wrap/rim/back terms for skin and foliage. All of its inputs come from data records. Exteriors take sun color, DALC and fog from the current weather's four time-of-day slots (Sunrise, Day, Sunset, Night), timed by the climate. Interiors take a static set from the cell's `XCLL` block and its Lighting Template. Color grading lives in **one tonemap shader** (`HDRTonemapBlendCinematic`). It does luminance-ratio Reinhard with a "White" term, adds bloom only to pixels below a "Receive Bloom Threshold", then applies saturation, luminance-based tint, brightness, contrast around the adapted luminance, `pow(x, 1/fGamma)`, and an optional fade. Its parameters come from a weather- or cell-selected ImageSpace (IMGS), with ImageSpace Modifier (IMAD) instances stacked on top. There is no LUT. These facts matter for anyone modding or reimplementing the engine because every major mod attaches at a specific seam. Data mods rewrite the records. SKSE plugins (Light Placer, Intellightent, Shadow Boost) drive the engine's own light objects. Community Shaders replaces the HLSL and turns opaque shading into clustered-forward plus a deferred composite. ENB intercepts D3D11 and replaces the tonemap draw outright. ReShade only sees the final LDR frame. The most-repeated piece of modding lore, "4 lights per object", conflicts with the decompiled SE shader setup, which supports 7. That conflict remains unresolved.

## How to read the confidence labels

The underlying research separated sources by how close they sit to the binary, and this report keeps those labels. **[CODE]** means read directly in reverse-engineered engine C++ or headers (Nukem9's `skyrimse-test` decompilation, CommonLibSSE-NG/CommonLibVR headers with Address Library IDs) or in xEdit's record definitions, which are the de facto binary spec for plugin data. **[RECON]** means read in Community Shaders' hand-reconstructed HLSL of the vanilla SE shaders. The earliest commits (Feb 2023 for `Lighting.hlsl`, Jul 2023 and Aug 2024 for `ISHDR.hlsl`) are transcribed from decompiled bytecode and still carry names like `UnknownPerFrame2[22]`. They are faithful in intent, but they are not Bethesda source. **[DOC]** means designer or community documentation (Creation Kit wiki, STEP INI guides, mod pages). **[FOLKLORE]** means unsourced forum or mod-page claims about engine behaviour. **[INFERENCE]** means a derivation by the researchers or by this report. For LE (DX9, `TESV.exe`), **no decompiled lighting or tonemap shader was found**, so every claim about LE shader math is at best inference.

## A forward renderer with one deferred trick: the shadow mask

**[CODE] SE lighting is forward and per-draw.** `BSLightingShader::SetupGeometry` decodes `lightCount = (rawTechnique >> 3) & 0b111` and `shadowLightCount = (rawTechnique >> 6) & 0b111` from the raw technique ID. It writes both into the pixel-shader constant `NumLightNumShadowLight`, then fills `DirLightDirection/Color`, the `DirectionalAmbient` 3x4 matrix and `PointLightPosition[7]` / `PointLightColor[7]` from the render pass's light array ([Nukem9 BSLightingShader.cpp](https://github.com/Nukem9/skyrimse-test/blob/master/skyrim64_test/src/patches/TES/BSShader/Shaders/BSLightingShader.cpp)). Light count is therefore part of the permutation key, the classic multi-permutation forward design.

**[CODE] Shadows are the deferred part.** Before the main pass, Utility shader permutations `RENDER_SHADOWMASK` (sun cascades), `RENDER_SHADOWMASKSPOT`, `RENDER_SHADOWMASKPB` (paraboloid) and `RENDER_SHADOWMASKDPB` (dual paraboloid) read scene depth and the shadow-map array. They write a screen-space `kSHADOW_MASK` target with one shadow caster per RGBA channel ([CS Utility.hlsl](https://github.com/doodlum/skyrim-community-shaders/blob/dev/package/Shaders/Utility.hlsl); [CommonLibVR BSShaderRenderTargets.h](https://github.com/alandtse/CommonLibVR/blob/8f4205da56f01cbe98557422c008a5719c180fb9/include/RE/B/BSShaderRenderTargets.h)). When a draw is shadowed and has the `DEFSHADOW` flag, the engine binds the mask at **slot t14** and writes `VPOSOffset = (1/width, 1/height)`. The pixel shader then reads it at `SV_Position.xy * VPOSOffset`. The sun uses `.x`, and shadowed point light *i* uses `shadowMask[ShadowLightMaskSelect[i]]` ([Nukem9](https://github.com/Nukem9/skyrimse-test/blob/master/skyrim64_test/src/patches/TES/BSShader/Shaders/BSLightingShader.cpp); [CS Lighting.hlsl](https://github.com/doodlum/skyrim-community-shaders/blob/dev/package/Shaders/Lighting.hlsl)). SE's main pass also writes normals, motion vectors and SSR/TAA masks (`kNORMAL_TAAMASK_SSRMASK`, `kMOTION_VECTOR`) to feed SAO, SSR, TAA and volumetric lighting, but **it never accumulates lights in screen space**.

**[CODE] Pass order.** Two accumulators exist, `ZPrePassAccumulator` and `MainPassAccumulator`. `FinishAccumulating_Standard_PreResolveDepth` gives the main-pass order as RenderBatches, LowAniso, RenderGrass, RenderNoShadowGroup, RenderLODObjects, RenderLODLand, RenderSky, RenderSkyClouds, normal decals, BlendedDecals and RenderWaterStencil, then SSR plus downsample image-space passes. When early-Z is off, the main pass depth-tests `EQUAL` against the prepass ([Nukem9 BSShaderAccumulator.cpp](https://github.com/Nukem9/skyrimse-test/blob/master/skyrim64_test/src/patches/TES/BSShader/BSShaderAccumulator.cpp)). Community Shaders' hook offsets in `Main::Render` (`RelocationID(35560, 36559)`) give the top-level frame: shadow maps at +0x2EC, depth at +0x395, precipitation occlusion at +0x3A1, world at +0x831 SE / +0x841 AE, and first person at +0x944 / +0x954. Inside the world render (`RelocationID(99938, 106583)`), opaque `RenderBatches` start at +0x8E/+0x84 and `BlendedDecals` sits at +0x319/+0x308 ([CS Deferred.cpp](https://github.com/doodlum/skyrim-community-shaders/blob/dev/src/Deferred.cpp)). **[INFERENCE]** The end-to-end SE pipeline is: shadow maps, depth prepass, shadow-mask resolve, forward opaque, grass/LOD/sky/clouds/decals, water stencil, SSR/SAO/volumetrics, water, alpha and effects, first person, then HDR tonemap, TAA and DoF. The ordering of alpha-blended passes after water was not traced in code.

**LE versus SE.** **[DOC]** LE is DX9 and SE is DX11 ([r/skyrimmods DXVK thread](https://reddit.rtrace.io/r/skyrimmods/comments/etmec6/sle_great_performance_gains_through_dxvk)). LE's deferred-shadow path (`bDeferredShadows`) was added in official patch 1.6, and `bShadowsOnGrass` / `bDrawLandShadows` depend on it ([STEP SkyrimPrefs Display](https://stepmodifications.org/wiki/Guide:SkyrimPrefs_INI/Display)). **[FOLKLORE]** A 2016 forum thread calls LE "the forward renderer used in OG" and says SE has "some 8000 odd individual shaders" ([Nexus forums](https://forums.nexusmods.com/topic/5018100-why-is-enb-support-for-sse-limited)). **[INFERENCE]** SE's normal/motion-vector outputs, SSR, SAO, TAA and volumetric god rays are SE additions; the familiar "ported from Fallout 4" framing was not sourced. LE's per-draw light encoding, constant layout and pass order are unverified.

**[CODE] Community Shaders' deferred conversion.** CS calls `Deferred::StartDeferred()` at the start of opaque `RenderBatches` and `EndDeferred()` / `DeferredPasses()` after `BlendedDecals`. It reuses vanilla render-target slots (`kINDIRECT`, `kRAWINDIRECT*`) as G-buffer storage, and `DeferredCompositeCS.hlsl` reads specular, albedo, normal-roughness, masks, depth, reflectance and sky IBL ([CS Deferred.cpp](https://github.com/doodlum/skyrim-community-shaders/blob/dev/src/Deferred.cpp); [DeepWiki deferred architecture](https://deepwiki.com/doodlum/skyrim-community-shaders/3.1-deferred-rendering-architecture)). Even so, CS keeps direct lighting forward (clustered). The deferred pass only moves ambient, specular and indirect composition into screen space.

## BSLightingShader packs light counts into its permutation key

`BSLightingShader` is the uber-shader for nearly all lit opaque and alpha-tested geometry: statics, actors, skin, hair, eyes, terrain and LOD. **[CODE]** A 32-bit technique descriptor selects each permutation. **Bits 24 to 29 hold the sub-technique**: 0 None, 1 Envmap, 2 Glowmap, 3 Parallax, 4 Facegen, 5 FacegenRGBTint, 6 Hair, 7 ParallaxOcc, 8 MTLand, 9 LODLand, 10 Snow, 11 MultilayerParallax, 12 TreeAnim, 13 LODObjects, 14 MultiIndexTriShapeSnow, 15 LODObjectHD, 16 Eye, 17 Cloud, 18 LODLandNoise, 19 MTLandLODBlend ([Nukem9 BSLightingShader.h](https://github.com/Nukem9/skyrimse-test/blob/master/skyrim64_test/src/patches/TES/BSShader/Shaders/BSLightingShader.h); [CS ShaderCache.h](https://github.com/doodlum/skyrim-community-shaders/blob/dd2677fc4020db1da91b39cebef3dbfbe8913983/src/ShaderCache.h)). CS marks 10 (Snow) and 17 (Cloud) as unused. That claim is asserted, not verified. **Bits 0 to 23 are feature flags**: `VC`, `SKINNED`, `MODELSPACENORMALS`, then bits 9 to 23 `SPECULAR`, `SOFT_LIGHTING`, `RIM_LIGHTING`, `BACK_LIGHTING`, `SHADOW_DIR`, `DEFSHADOW`, `PROJECTED_UV` (it means `DEPTH_WRITE_DECALS` on Hair), `ANISO_LIGHTING`, `AMBIENT_SPECULAR`, `WORLD_MAP`, `BASE_OBJECT_IS_SNOW`, `DO_ALPHA_TEST`, `SNOW`, `CHARACTER_LIGHT` and `ADDITIONAL_ALPHA_MASK`. Each set bit becomes an HLSL `#define` ([CommonLibVR BSLightingShader.h](https://github.com/alandtse/CommonLibVR/blob/8f4205da56f01cbe98557422c008a5719c180fb9/include/RE/B/BSLightingShader.h)).

**A naming conflict sits in bits 3 to 8.** CommonLib names them `kLightCount1..6` as if they were six one-hot flags. Nukem9's own header comments `RAW_FLAG_LIGHTCOUNT1..6 // Probably not used`, and CS calls them "unused by vanilla" and reuses bit 3 for TruePBR and bit 4 for Deferred. Yet Nukem9's decompiled `SetupGeometry` reads them as **two 3-bit fields** (point-light count in bits 3 to 5, shadowed count in bits 6 to 8), and the debug name format `"%dSh%d "` prints both ([Nukem9 BSLightingShader.cpp](https://github.com/Nukem9/skyrimse-test/blob/master/skyrim64_test/src/patches/TES/BSShader/Shaders/BSLightingShader.cpp)). Both views hold at different levels. **[CODE]** `GetPixelTechnique` strips the count bits before the pixel-shader lookup, so they matter only in the raw descriptor and the constant buffer, not in compiled PS variants. The same derivation code produces the raw, vertex and pixel keys. It computes `raw = Technique - 0x4800002D`, rewrites LODLandNoise to LODLand when `bLODLandscapeNoise` is off, and clears ParallaxOcc when `bEnableParallaxOcclusion` is off. The vertex key keeps only sub-type, VC, SKINNED, MODELSPACENORMALS, PROJECTED_UV and WORLD_MAP, plus SPECULAR if any view-dependent term is on. The pixel key clears SKINNED unless model-space normals are on and always ORs in VC.

**[CODE] NIF flags drive the descriptor.** `BSLightingShaderProperty` stores SLSF1 and SLSF2 as one 64-bit `EShaderPropertyFlag`. Examples: Specular bit 0, Model_Space_Normals bit 12, Soft_Lighting bit 57, Rim_Lighting bit 58, Back_Lighting bit 59, Anisotropic_Lighting bit 53. Bit 40 is named **CharacterLighting** in-engine but "Assume_Shadowmask" in nif.xml ([CommonLibSSE-NG BSShaderProperty.h](https://github.com/CharmedBaryon/CommonLibSSE-NG/blob/main/include/RE/B/BSShaderProperty.h); [nif.xml](https://github.com/niftools/nifxml/blob/develop/nif.xml)). **[INFERENCE]** The material setup writes `LightingEffectParams.x = fSubSurfaceLightRolloff` and `.y = fRimLightPower`. Combined with nif.xml's vague labels, that means NifSkope's **"Lighting Effect 1" is the soft-light rolloff and "Lighting Effect 2" is the rim power**, "Glossiness" is the specular exponent, and "Specular Strength" is a linear scale.

**[CODE] CS reuses the vanilla define builder.** It calls `GetLightingShaderDefines` at `RELOCATION_ID(101631, 108698)`, then appends `DEFERRED`, `TRUE_PBR` and feature defines. In `State::ModifyShaderLookup` it collapses permutations by stripping AmbientSpecular, ShadowDir, DefShadow, CharacterLight and the snow flags from the PS key and testing the real descriptor at runtime instead ([CS ShaderCache.cpp](https://github.com/doodlum/skyrim-community-shaders/blob/dd2677fc4020db1da91b39cebef3dbfbe8913983/src/ShaderCache.cpp); [CS State.cpp](https://github.com/doodlum/skyrim-community-shaders/blob/dd2677fc4020db1da91b39cebef3dbfbe8913983/src/State.cpp)). For a reimplementation, this is the practical lesson: Bethesda's permutation explosion can be replaced by runtime branching on a descriptor uniform with no visible change.

## The per-pixel equation is gamma-space Lambert plus unnormalized Blinn-Phong

**[CODE] CPU-side constant feeds.** `PointLightColor[i] = NiLight.dimmer * BSLight.lodDimmer * NiLight.diffuse`, which is zeroed under `bLiteBrite`. `PointLightPosition[i].w = NiLight.GetSpecularColor().r`: **Bethesda stores the light radius in Gamebryo's specular-red slot**, divided by world scale when rendering in model space. `SpecularColor = kSpecularColor * fSpecularColorScale` with `.w = fSpecularPower`, and `EmitColor = emit * fEmitColorScale`. `DirLightColor` is `sunLight.dimmer * diffuse` times an unidentified global scalar at `qword_1431F5810 + 224` ([Nukem9 BSLightingShader.cpp](https://github.com/Nukem9/skyrimse-test/blob/master/skyrim64_test/src/patches/TES/BSShader/Shaders/BSLightingShader.cpp)). `NiPointLight` carries constant, linear and quadratic attenuation fields at 0x140, and **the shader ignores them** ([CommonLibSSE-NG NiPointLight.h](https://github.com/CharmedBaryon/CommonLibSSE-NG/blob/main/include/RE/N/NiPointLight.h)). `NiLight::ambient` is likewise unused for point lights, which is why Light Placer and CS pack flags, cutoff and FormID bits into it ([LightPlacer LightData.cpp](https://github.com/powerof3/LightPlacer/blob/master/src/LightData.cpp)).

**[RECON] The terms.** The point-light loop runs `i < min(7, numLights)`. It computes `atten = 1 - saturate(dist/radius)^2` and shadows light *i* only if `i < numShadowLights`. Diffuse is `lightColor * saturate(N·L) * atten`. Specular is `pow(saturate(N·H), SpecularColor.w)` with **no N·L factor, no Fresnel and no normalization**. It is then multiplied by the gloss map (normal-map alpha, or spec-map `.x` with model-space normals), `specularLODFade` and `SpecularColor.rgb`. Soft lighting is a wrap band beyond the terminator, `saturate(smoothstep(saturate((r + N·L)/(1 + r))) - smoothstep(saturate(N·L)))`, tinted by the soft-light texture. Rim is `pow(1 - saturate(N·V), rimPower) * saturate(V·-L)`. Back lighting is `saturate(-N·L) * backLightTex`. **Shadows multiply only Lambert and specular.** Soft, rim and back use the unshadowed light color, kept as `nsLightColor` in the original reconstruction and as `softShadow` versus `detailedShadow` in current CS ([CS original Lighting.hlsl](https://github.com/doodlum/skyrim-community-shaders/blob/5a530ba2bd10d6dc22b476244b9e162ec93c3ca3/package/Shaders/Lighting.hlsl); [CS-now LightingEval.hlsli](https://github.com/doodlum/skyrim-community-shaders/blob/dd2677fc4020db1da91b39cebef3dbfbe8913983/package/Shaders/Common/LightingEval.hlsli)). Hair `ANISO_LIGHTING` uses a Kajiya-like `1 - |dot(AN,L) - dot(AN,H)|` scaled by `0.7 * max(0, L.z)`. Snow `SPARKLE` adds a thresholded noise glint. **Vanilla has no dedicated skin SSS.** Skin "subsurface" is just `SOFT_LIGHTING` with the rolloff and the soft-light texture.

Assembled, the vanilla equation reads as follows **[INFERENCE from RECON]**, with `a_i = 1 - (d_i/r_i)^2`, `S_d` = mask `.x` and `S_i` = the selected mask channel:

```
D_lights = L_dir*S_d*sat(N·L_dir) + Σ_i a_i*L_i*S_i*sat(N·L_i)
         + Σ_{dir,i} a_i*L_i(unshadowed)*(soft(N·L)*T_soft + rim(N,V,L)*T_soft + sat(-N·L)*T_back)
D_total  = DALC(N) + Emit*Glow + D_lights + CharLight
C        = VC.rgb * [HairTint] * Albedo.rgb * D_total
         + D_total * EnvCube(reflect) * envMask                    (ENVMAP / EYE / MLP)
Spec     = (Σ a_i*L_i*S_i*(N·H_i)^gloss) * glossMap * SpecLODFade * SpecColor*SpecStrength
         + AmbSpecTint * sat(DALC(N, w=0.15)) * (1 - N·V)^fresnelPow  (AMBIENT_SPECULAR)
out      = fog/clamp(C) + Spec
```

Several consequences surprise people. **Emissive is added before the albedo multiply**, so vanilla glow is tinted by the diffuse texture and vertex color. **Envmap reflections are scaled by received diffuse light** (`D_total * envColor`), not by a Fresnel or specular term, so dark rooms produce dark reflections. That explains why CS Dynamic Cubemaps changes the look so much ([CS original Lighting.hlsl](https://github.com/doodlum/skyrim-community-shaders/blob/5a530ba2bd10d6dc22b476244b9e162ec93c3ca3/package/Shaders/Lighting.hlsl)). Hair tint is `lerp(1, TintColor, vertexColor.g)`. The reconstruction also passes the *unnormalized* light vector to the point-light soft-light call. That could be a vanilla quirk or a transcription artifact; it is unverified.

**[RECON + CODE] DALC is an affine function of the normal, not an ambient cube.** The shader computes `ambient = mul(DirectionalAmbient /*row_major float3x4*/, float4(N, 1))`. The CPU stores `BSShaderManager::State::directionalAmbientTransform` and pre-rotates it into model space for model-space draws ([Nukem9](https://github.com/Nukem9/skyrimse-test/blob/master/skyrim64_test/src/patches/TES/BSShader/Shaders/BSLightingShader.cpp)). The data has six axis colors (X+, X-, Y+, Y-, Z+, Z-) per DALC block ([CK Lighting Template](https://ck.uesp.net/wiki/Lighting_Template)). **[INFERENCE]** A 3x4 affine map hits the six axis colors exactly when `bias = Σ(X+ + X-)/2` per axis and `slope = (X+ - X-)/2`. Off-axis normals then interpolate *linearly* in N, which is closer to first-order spherical harmonics than to Half-Life 2's squared-weight ambient cube. The CPU conversion code was not located.

**Ambient specular is a three-way conflict.** **[RECON]** With `AMBIENT_SPECULAR`, the original reconstruction adds a Schlick-like rim, `AmbSpecTint * sat(DALC(N, 0.15)) * (1 - N·V)^p`. **[CODE]** Nukem9 notes a vanilla bug: the constant is set in `SetupMaterial` instead of `SetupGeometry` and is "incorrectly label[led] as PerMaterial" ([Nukem9](https://github.com/Nukem9/skyrimse-test/blob/master/skyrim64_test/src/patches/TES/BSShader/Shaders/BSLightingShader.cpp)). **[DOC]** The CK wiki says Specular and Fresnel Power "do nothing; never fully implemented" ([CK Lighting Template](https://ck.uesp.net/wiki/Lighting_Template)), and SSE Engine Fixes ships `BSLightingAmbientSpecular` to fix "lighting template Directional Ambient Specular & Fresnel Power are broken" ([SSE Engine Fixes](https://www.nexusmods.com/skyrimspecialedition/mods/17230)). **[INFERENCE]** The likeliest reconciliation is that the term exists in the shader, but the per-material update bug makes the per-cell or per-weather values stale or wrong, so designers saw no effect. Current CS strips the flag and no longer evaluates the term ([CS State.cpp](https://github.com/doodlum/skyrim-community-shaders/blob/dd2677fc4020db1da91b39cebef3dbfbe8913983/src/State.cpp)). CS's own comments disagree with each other about whether Fresnel power sits in `.z` or `.w`.

**[RECON + CODE] The character light is a screen-space hack.** With `CHARACTER_LIGHT`, the shader adds a grayscale term, `(saturate(V·N)*Primary + Secondary*saturate(dot((0.1644, -0.9864), N.yz))) * min(MaxLum, Luminance * tex.x)`, before the albedo multiply. `tex` is a render target bound at slot 11 and sampled in screen space. The parameters come from `BSShaderManager::State::characterLightParams {Primary, Secondary, Luminance, MaxLuminance}`, copied only when `characterLightEnabled` ([CS-now Lighting.hlsl](https://github.com/doodlum/skyrim-community-shaders/blob/dd2677fc4020db1da91b39cebef3dbfbe8913983/package/Shaders/Lighting.hlsl); [CommonLibVR BSShaderManager.h](https://github.com/alandtse/CommonLibVR/blob/8f4205da56f01cbe98557422c008a5719c180fb9/include/RE/B/BSShaderManager.h)). **[DOC]** Players know it as the NPC "subtle glow" that `cl off` disables, and ENB's `DisableFakeLights` targets it ([Disable Character Lighting](https://nexusmods.com/skyrimspecialedition/mods/38624)). Which render target feeds slot 11, and when the engine enables the light, are unconfirmed.

**[CODE + RECON] Fog is per-vertex, distance-only, with no height term.** `TechUpdateFogConstants` packs `FogParam = {near/(far-near), 1/(far-near), power, max}`. The vertex shader computes `f = min(max, pow(saturate(dist*FogParam.y - FogParam.x), power))` and `fogColor = lerp(nearColor, farColor, f)`. The pixel shader then blends `lerp(color, fogColor, f)` ([Nukem9](https://github.com/Nukem9/skyrimse-test/blob/master/skyrim64_test/src/patches/TES/BSShader/Shaders/BSLightingShader.cpp); [CS-now Lighting.hlsl](https://github.com/doodlum/skyrim-community-shaders/blob/dd2677fc4020db1da91b39cebef3dbfbe8913983/package/Shaders/Lighting.hlsl)). Effect shaders fog by blend mode: normal blend lerps, additive blend fades to black (`color*(1-f)`), multiplicative blend fades to identity ([CS Effect.hlsl](https://github.com/doodlum/skyrim-community-shaders/blob/dd2677fc4020db1da91b39cebef3dbfbe8913983/package/Shaders/Effect.hlsl)). Grass is lit per vertex as `albedo * (dirDiffuse * lerp(sunShadow, 1, ShadowClampValue) + ambient)` and carries only a scalar from the fog constants ([Nukem9 RunGrass.hlsl](https://github.com/Nukem9/skyrimse-test/blob/328916305165a46c4e4b527735bbcfd46b09a0ca/Shaders/RunGrass.hlsl)). That matches the modder note that "DALC does not affect grass" ([Guide to Weather Creation](https://www.nexusmods.com/skyrimspecialedition/mods/4704)). Water stores fog power in `NormalsScale.w` ([CS Water.hlsl](https://github.com/doodlum/skyrim-community-shaders/blob/dd2677fc4020db1da91b39cebef3dbfbe8913983/package/Shaders/Water.hlsl)). Height fog is a CS feature, not vanilla. The relative order of specular, fog and the `fLightingOutputColourClamp*` output clamps differs between the 2023 reconstruction and current CS, and an unidentified `UnknownPerFrame2[22].yz` participates. Treat vanilla's exact tail as unresolved.

**[RECON] Gamma.** The reconstruction multiplies raw texture colors by raw light colors and adds lights with no `pow()` anywhere. The only gamma constant is `FrameParams.x` ("inverse fGamma"), applied at output ([CS FrameBuffer.hlsli](https://github.com/doodlum/skyrim-community-shaders/blob/dd2677fc4020db1da91b39cebef3dbfbe8913983/package/Shaders/Common/FrameBuffer.hlsli)). CS models vanilla irradiance with an empirical **"Skyrim gamma" of 1.6**, not 2.2 (`SkyrimGammaToLinear = pow(x, 1.6)`). Its optional Linear Lighting defaults to exponent 1.8 for light, color, ambient and emissive and 1.97 for fog, and divides Lambert by π ([CS Color.hlsli](https://github.com/doodlum/skyrim-community-shaders/blob/dd2677fc4020db1da91b39cebef3dbfbe8913983/package/Shaders/Common/Color.hlsli); [CS LinearLighting.h](https://github.com/doodlum/skyrim-community-shaders/blob/dd2677fc4020db1da91b39cebef3dbfbe8913983/src/Features/LinearLighting.h)). **Even with Linear Lighting off, current CS is not bit-exact vanilla.** It converts lit diffuse to "linear" with pow 1.6, adds specular and cubemap reflection, and converts back before fog. Whether vanilla samples textures through sRGB SRVs was not checked and would need RenderDoc.

## Seven point lights per draw, four shadow channels per scene

**[CODE] Two object layers represent a light.** The scene-graph `NiLight` (0x140) holds `ambient`, `diffuse`, `radius` (an `NiPoint3`, of which the shader reads only `.x`) and `fade` (Gamebryo's dimmer). The renderer wrapper `BSLight` holds `luminance`, `lodDimmer`, the lit-geometry list, `portalStrict`, `affectLand`, `affectWater`, room and portal arrays, the portal graph and lens-flare data ([CommonLibVR NiLight.h](https://github.com/alandtse/CommonLibVR/blob/8f4205da56f01cbe98557422c008a5719c180fb9/include/RE/N/NiLight.h); [CommonLibVR BSLight.h](https://github.com/alandtse/CommonLibVR/blob/8f4205da56f01cbe98557422c008a5719c180fb9/include/RE/B/BSLight.h)). Shadow casters subclass `BSShadowLight` (0x560), whose `maskIndex` at 0x520 is the "shadow mask channel index (0..3 for R/G/B/A) ... engine does not bounds-check" ([CommonLibVR BSShadowLight.h](https://github.com/alandtse/CommonLibVR/blob/8f4205da56f01cbe98557422c008a5719c180fb9/include/RE/B/BSShadowLight.h)). The subclasses are `BSShadowDirectionalLight` (sun, with three cascade split pairs), `BSShadowFrustumLight` (spot, with FOV, falloff and near/far planes) and `BSShadowParabolicLight`, which covers both hemi (single paraboloid) and omni (dual paraboloid). The singleton `ShadowSceneNode` (`BSShaderManager::State::shadowSceneNode[0]`) owns `activeLights`, `activeShadowLights`, add/remove queues, `sunLight`, `cloudLight` and `sunShadowDirLight` ([CommonLibVR ShadowSceneNode.h](https://github.com/alandtse/CommonLibVR/blob/8f4205da56f01cbe98557422c008a5719c180fb9/include/RE/S/ShadowSceneNode.h)).

The CK describes all spotlights as shadow-casting ([CK tutorial](https://ck.uesp.net/wiki/Bethesda_Tutorial_Lights_and_FX)). Its Light page says Falloff Exponent "only applies to spotlights", consistent with the point-light shader having no exponent, and calls Portal Strict "Required for shadow lights" ([CK Light](https://ck.uesp.net/wiki/Light)). LIGH bits 14 "Inverse Square" and 15 "Linear" are CS extensions that xEdit labels only when targeting CS ([xEdit TES5 defs](https://github.com/TES5Edit/TES5Edit/blob/dev-4.1.6/Core/wbDefinitionsTES5.pas)).

**[CODE] Per-draw assignment.** A `BSRenderPass` (0x48) carries `numLights`, `numShadowLights` and `BSLight** sceneLights`. **Slot 0 is the directional light, and point lights fill slots 1 to 7, with shadowed lights first** ([CommonLibVR BSRenderPass.h](https://github.com/alandtse/CommonLibVR/blob/8f4205da56f01cbe98557422c008a5719c180fb9/include/RE/B/BSRenderPass.h)). The vanilla setup asserts `ShadowLightCount <= 4, "Shader only expects shadow selector data to fit in a FLOAT4"` ([Nukem9](https://github.com/Nukem9/skyrimse-test/blob/master/skyrim64_test/src/patches/TES/BSShader/Shaders/BSLightingShader.cpp)). Each shader property keeps a persistent `BSShaderPropertyLightData { lights, lightListFence, activeLightMask, lightListChanged }`. The property's render-pass builder takes `(technique, numLights, light0..light3)`, so only **four** light pointers are passed directly at creation ([CommonLibVR BSShaderProperty.h](https://github.com/alandtse/CommonLibVR/blob/8f4205da56f01cbe98557422c008a5719c180fb9/include/RE/B/BSShaderProperty.h)). Ranking almost certainly uses `BSLight::GetLuminance(targetPos)` at `RelocationID(101303, 108292)`, cached in `BSLight::luminance`. CS's Inverse Square Lighting hooks exactly this function so that physical falloff changes which lights win ([CS InverseSquareLighting.cpp](https://github.com/doodlum/skyrim-community-shaders/blob/dev/src/Features/InverseSquareLighting.cpp)). **The function that fills, sorts and truncates the per-object list was not decompiled.** Light Limit Fix's `ValidLight` patch sites (SE 100994, 100997, 101296) point to where it lives.

**[INFERENCE] Most likely reconciliation.** The SE shader and descriptor allow 7 point lights per draw, so 7 is the structural ceiling. The widely observed "4" likely comes from one or more of: LE-era lore carried forward, the four-pointer `light0..light3` pass-builder signature, and the four-channel shadow mask. Code confirms the last two exist, but none of the sources establishes which one causes the visible four-light flicker in SE. Kesta's "6" may be an off-by-one or may count something else. A test cell with 5 to 8 non-shadow omnis on a single unsplit mesh, inspected in RenderDoc for `NumLightNumShadowLight`, would settle it.

**[CODE + DOC] Shadow casters per scene.** The CK says "only four can be visible at any given time" ([CK tutorial](https://ck.uesp.net/wiki/Bethesda_Tutorial_Lights_and_FX)). Forum lore says the engine "starts disabling lights if there are more" ([Nexus forums](https://forums.nexusmods.com/topic/1015400-maximum-number-of-lights)), and Intellightent says the game "arbitrarily choose[s] which 4 will be active" ([STEP](https://stepmodifications.org/forum/topic/21445-intellightent-by-meh321/)). This matches the four-channel mask and the 0..3 `maskIndex`. **[INFERENCE]** In exteriors the sun reads `.x`, which plausibly leaves fewer channels for point shadow lights. That would fit STEP's practical "no more than 2" budget in some setups, but it is unconfirmed. Inactive shadow lights carry `maskIndex == 255` ([CS LightLimitFix.cpp](https://github.com/doodlum/skyrim-community-shaders/blob/dev/src/Features/LightLimitFix.cpp)). **[CODE]** The game setting `iMagicLightMaxCount` caps simultaneous magic lights; it was 4 per the old LLF page, and LLF sets it to `INT32_MAX` ([LLF pre-1.4](https://nexusmods.com/skyrimspecialedition/mods/99548)).

**Fade.** **[CODE]** Effective intensity is `fade * lodDimmer * diffuse`. The engine globals `lightFadeStart` / `lightFadeEnd` sit at `RelocationID(527668, 414582)` and `(527669, 414583)` ([CS LightLimitFix.cpp](https://github.com/doodlum/skyrim-community-shaders/blob/dev/src/Features/LightLimitFix.cpp)). **[DOC]** `fLightLODStartFade` (default 1000) and `fLightLODRange` (500) set the defaults. A cell's Light Fade Begin/End override them, and a value of 0 falls back to the INI. These fades do "not affect shadow casters" ([STEP SSE Display](https://stepmodifications.org/wiki/Guide:SSE_SkyrimPrefs_INI/Display); [CK Cell Lighting](https://ck.uesp.net/wiki/Cell_Lighting); [CK Lighting Template](https://ck.uesp.net/wiki/Lighting_Template)).

**[CODE] Sun and cascades.** The sun is `Sky::sun->light`, an `NiDirectionalLight`, with a second `cloudLight` whose use is unverified ([CommonLibVR Sun.h](https://github.com/alandtse/CommonLibVR/blob/8f4205da56f01cbe98557422c008a5719c180fb9/include/RE/S/Sun.h)). It casts through `BSShadowDirectionalLight` with **up to 3 cascades** (`iNumSplits`, max 3) in one `Texture2DArray`, plus up to 4 per-actor "focus" shadow maps (`iNumFocusShadow`, default 4) ([CS ShadowmapCascadeRasterizerFix.h](https://github.com/doodlum/skyrim-community-shaders/blob/dev/src/EngineFixes/ShadowmapCascadeRasterizerFix.h); [CommonLibVR BSShadowDirectionalLight.h](https://github.com/alandtse/CommonLibVR/blob/8f4205da56f01cbe98557422c008a5719c180fb9/include/RE/B/BSShadowDirectionalLight.h)). CS documents two vanilla cascade bugs: **one rasterizer state is shared across all cascades**, and **each cascade's near plane equals the previous far plane, which already includes `fSplitOverlap`**, doubling the overlap and causing gaps ([CS ShadowmapCascadeCullingFix.h](https://github.com/doodlum/skyrim-community-shaders/blob/dev/src/EngineFixes/ShadowmapCascadeCullingFix.h)). All cascades share one `fShadowDistance` and `iShadowMapResolution`. Shadow Boost proves this by rescaling only `fShadowDistance` (`RELOCATION_ID(513793, 391845)`) each frame and calling `UpdateShadowDistanceAndInteriorFlag` (`98978, 105631`) ([skyrim-shadow-boost](https://github.com/doodlum/skyrim-shadow-boost/blob/main/src/ShadowBoost.cpp)). **[CODE + DOC]** The shadow direction is not the visible sun. CS SkySync "fixes the mismatch between the positions of the sun and moons and the lighting direction" ([CS SkySync.h](https://github.com/doodlum/skyrim-community-shaders/blob/dev/src/Features/SkySync.h)), and EVLaS describes night shadows following "an invisible sun that moves backwards" ([EVLaS](https://www.nexusmods.com/skyrimspecialedition/mods/63725)). Sun-shadow direction updates are throttled through `sunVector` / `lastSunVector` / `sunUpdate`, linked to `fSunShadowUpdateTime` / `fSunUpdateThreshold` ([Nukem9 settings.cpp](https://github.com/Nukem9/skyrimse-test/blob/master/skyrim64_test/src/patches/settings.cpp)). Their vanilla defaults were not verified. **[DOC]** The STEP guide calls `iShadowSplitCount` placebo, and `iBlurDeferredShadowMask` of at least 3 hides the striping that low `fShadowBiasScale` causes ([STEP SkyrimPrefs Display](https://stepmodifications.org/wiki/Guide:SkyrimPrefs_INI/Display)).

## Weathers and Lighting Templates feed the same shader constants

Interior and exterior lighting run **the same shader permutations**. Only the source of the constants and the `SHADOW_DIR` flag differ. No `INTERIOR` define exists in the lighting permutation list ([CS Permutation.hlsli](https://github.com/doodlum/skyrim-community-shaders/blob/dd2677fc4020db1da91b39cebef3dbfbe8913983/package/Shaders/Common/Permutation.hlsli)).

**Exteriors: WTHR.** **[CODE]** Every per-time field has **exactly four slots**, `ColorTime` = Sunrise, Day, Sunset, Night. **The "Early/Late Sunrise/Sunset" slots exist only in Fallout 4+**, gated by `IsFO4Plus` in xEdit ([CommonLibSSE-NG TESWeather.h](https://github.com/CharmedBaryon/CommonLibSSE-NG/blob/main/include/RE/T/TESWeather.h); [xEdit common defs](https://github.com/TES5Edit/TES5Edit/blob/dev-4.1.6/Core/wbDefinitionsCommon.pas)). NAM0 holds 17 colors x 4 slots, in this order: 0 Sky-Upper, 1 Fog Near, 2 Unknown, 3 Ambient, 4 Sunlight, 5 Sun, 6 Stars, 7 Sky-Lower, 8 Horizon, 9 Effect Lighting, 10/11 Cloud LOD Diffuse/Ambient, 12 Fog Far, 13 Sky Statics, 14 Water Multiplier, 15/16 Sun/Moon Glare. Later entries are gated on form version 31, 35 and 37 ([UESP WTHR](https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Skyrim_Mod:Mod_File_Format/WTHR)). The blended per-frame result lives in `Sky::skyColor[17]`. CS's SkySync hooks `Sky::UpdateColors` (`RelocationID 25686/26233`) and scales `skyColor[kSunlight]`, which confirms that **index 4 Sunlight drives the sun's directional color** ([CommonLibSSE-NG Sky.h](https://github.com/CharmedBaryon/CommonLibSSE-NG/blob/main/include/RE/S/Sky.h); [CS SkySync.cpp](https://github.com/doodlum/skyrim-community-shaders/blob/dev/src/Features/SkySync.cpp)). **[DOC]** Weather authors report that NAM0 Ambient sets grass and tree-LOD color and that DALC "affects everything else" ([Guide to Weather Creation](https://www.nexusmods.com/skyrimspecialedition/mods/4704)). That fits the grass shader taking a separate `AmbientColor`.

**[CODE] DALC.** There are four blocks, in order Sunrise, Day, Sunset, Night. Each holds six axis colors, a Specular color and a Fresnel Power float, stored in-engine as `BGSDirectionalAmbientLightingColors { MaxMin<Color> x, y, z; specular; fresnelPower }` with max = +axis ([CommonLibSSE-NG BGSDirectionalAmbientLightingColors.h](https://github.com/CharmedBaryon/CommonLibSSE-NG/blob/main/include/RE/B/BGSDirectionalAmbientLightingColors.h)). The blended result sits in `Sky::directionalAmbientColors[3][2]`, `ambientSpecularTint` and `ambientSpecularFresnel`. These pass through `Sky_SetDirectionalAmbientColors` (`98989/105643`), the hook point CS uses ([CS Hooks.cpp](https://github.com/community-shaders/skyrim-community-shaders/blob/main/src/Hooks.cpp)).

**[CODE] Fog.** FNAM has **only Day and Night** entries (near, far, power, max), while fog colors have all four slots ([xEdit common defs](https://github.com/TES5Edit/TES5Edit/blob/dev-4.1.6/Core/wbDefinitionsCommon.pas)). The blended values land in `Sky::fogNear/fogFar/fogPower/fogClamp`. **[INFERENCE]** Fog distance probably blends Day to Night across the dawn and dusk windows while fog color passes through the Sunrise and Sunset keys.

**[CODE + DOC] Time of day.** CLMT TNAM stores Sunrise Begin/End and Sunset Begin/End as u8 values in 10-minute units. Vanilla uses **5:30, 10:00, 16:00 and 20:30** ([UESP CLMT](https://content2.uesp.net/wiki/Skyrim_Mod:Mod_File_Format/CLMT); [STEP ENB Timeofday](https://stepmodifications.org/wiki/SkyrimSE:ENBSeriesINI/Timeofday)). `Sky` carries `kUpdateSunriseBegin/End`, `kUpdateSunsetBegin/End` and `kUpdateColorsSunriseBegin/SunsetEnd` flags, so color updates are scheduled on those boundaries. CS SkySync reimplements `Sun::Update` "with the same float ops": the sun fades in from `midpoint(sunrise) - fSunAlphaTransTime/2` and out at `midpoint(sunset) + fSunAlphaTransTime/2` ([CS SkySync.cpp](https://github.com/doodlum/skyrim-community-shaders/blob/dev/src/Features/SkySync.cpp)). **The exact `Sky::UpdateColors` blend curve is not published anywhere.** **[INFERENCE]** The likely shape is Night, then Sunrise peaking at the sunrise midpoint (7:45 vanilla), then Day by Sunrise End, then Sunset peaking at 18:15, then Night by Sunset End. CS's editor uses a simplified 5-9 / 9-17 / 17-21 / 21-5 split and labels it "Simplified" in code, so it should not be treated as the engine formula. ENB's six periods (Dawn, Sunrise, Day, Sunset, Dusk, Night) are ENB's own model derived from TNAM, not engine data.

**[CODE] Weather transitions.** `Sky` holds `lastWeather`, `currentWeather` and `currentWeatherPct` (0 to 1), whose speed comes from DATA Trans Delta. CS treats the percentage as a lerp factor and warns that `lastWeather` "can be cleared before currentWeatherPct reaches 1.0" ([CommonLibSSE-NG Sky.h](https://github.com/CharmedBaryon/CommonLibSSE-NG/blob/main/include/RE/S/Sky.h)). How the stored Trans Delta byte (0 to 0.25) maps to the CK's "game hours" was not reconciled. Weather selection comes from the climate WLST unless a region RDWT applies, using region override and priority ([CK Region](https://ck.uesp.net/wiki/Region)).

**Interiors: XCLL and LGTM.** **[CODE]** CELL XCLL (92 bytes) mirrors LGTM DATA: ambient, directional color, fog near color, fog near/far, directional rotation XY and Z, directional fade, clip distance, fog power, a full DALC block, fog far color, fog max, light fade begin/end, and an **inherit bitfield** (0x001 ambient through 0x400 light fade). **There is no inherit bit for the DALC block** ([xEdit TES5 defs](https://github.com/TES5Edit/TES5Edit/blob/dev-4.1.6/Core/wbDefinitionsTES5.pas); [CommonLibSSE-NG InteriorData.h](https://github.com/CharmedBaryon/CommonLibSSE-NG/blob/main/include/RE/I/InteriorData.h)). **[INFERENCE]** A cell carrying its own XCLL therefore uses its own DALC regardless of template edits, which explains why template-only lighting mods sometimes "miss" cells. This should be verified in game. **[DOC]** The template's directional light is a cell-wide light "used to simulate bounced light". Its Ambient value only seeds "Set From Ambient" and "doesn't affect the actual lighting" ([CK Lighting Template](https://ck.uesp.net/wiki/Lighting_Template)). Vanilla interior directional light casts **no shadow map**, so it reaches every surface facing it, through walls. CS's Interior Sun exists to fix this and requires IsInterior + Show Sky + Use Sky Lighting + a custom bit-15 "Sunlight Shadows" flag ([CS InteriorSun.cpp](https://github.com/community-shaders/skyrim-community-shaders/blob/main/src/Features/InteriorSun.cpp)). **[CODE]** `TESObjectCELL::SetFogColor/Planes/Power` write `INTERIOR_DATA` only when the cell does not use sky lighting ([CommonLibSSE-NG TESObjectCELL.cpp](https://github.com/CharmedBaryon/CommonLibSSE-NG/blob/main/src/RE/T/TESObjectCELL.cpp)). Show Sky (0x80) plus Use Sky Lighting (0x100) pulls the weather's four-slot data, with XCCM selecting the region. Room bounds can carry their own LGTM and IMGS (XRMR flags 0x80/0x40), and `Sky` tracks `lightingTransition` timers between rooms.

**Three documentation conflicts to know about.** **[DOC versus CODE]** First, the CK Cell Lighting page calls Fog Max "Not used", while the template page documents it as a strength clamp and the shader applies `min(FogParam.w, ...)`. Trust the shader. Second, the CK describes the checkboxes as "check to override", while xEdit, CommonLib and CS name the stored bits "Inherits" ([CK Cell Lighting](https://ck.uesp.net/wiki/Cell_Lighting); [CS CellLightingWidget.cpp](https://github.com/community-shaders/skyrim-community-shaders/blob/main/src/CSEditor/Weather/CellLightingWidget.cpp)). The stored bit means "inherit", and the CK UI may show the inverse. Third, UESP types XCCM as a climate, while xEdit types it as a REGN.

**LE versus SE record differences.** **[CODE]** xEdit's `IsSSE` gates show SE adds WTHR HNAM (four VOLI volumetric-lighting forms, one per time slot, required in SE), WTHR GNAM (sun-glare LENS), LIGH LNAM (LENS), and the VOLI and LENS record types. WTHR, CLMT, LGTM, CELL and LIGH layouts are otherwise identical, so porting an SE weather to LE means dropping HNAM and GNAM ([xEdit TES5 defs](https://github.com/TES5Edit/TES5Edit/blob/dev-4.1.6/Core/wbDefinitionsTES5.pas)). God rays as a record (GDRY) exist only in Fallout 4.

## ImageSpace grades in a single tonemap pass

**[CODE] What an IMGS holds.** HNAM has nine HDR floats: Eye Adapt Speed, Bloom Blur Radius, Bloom Threshold, Bloom Scale, Receive Bloom Threshold, White, Sunlight Scale, Sky Scale, Eye Adapt Strength. CNAM has Saturation, Brightness and Contrast. TNAM has tint amount plus RGB. DNAM holds DoF strength, distance and range plus a packed sky/blur-radius u16. **There is no vignette and no LUT.** Fallout 4's IMGS adds `TX00 'LUT'`, and Skyrim's has no texture field at all ([xEdit TES5 defs](https://github.com/TES5Edit/TES5Edit/blob/dev-4.1.6/Core/wbDefinitionsTES5.pas); [xEdit FO4 defs](https://github.com/TES5Edit/TES5Edit/blob/dev-4.1.6/Core/wbDefinitionsFO4.pas); [CommonLibSSE-NG ImageSpaceData.h](https://github.com/CharmedBaryon/CommonLibSSE-NG/blob/main/include/RE/I/ImageSpaceData.h)). **[DOC]** CK defaults: Eye Adapt Speed 3, Strength 3, Bloom Blur Radius 7 ("cannot be turned off"), Bloom Threshold 0.6, Bloom Scale 0.5, Receive Bloom Threshold 0.15, White 0.15, Sunlight Scale 1.8 and Sky Scale 1.5 (both flagged "non-imagespace"), Saturation 0.9, Brightness 1.5, Contrast 1.1, Tint 0 ([CK ImageSpace](https://ck.uesp.net/wiki/ImageSpace)).

**[CODE] Field-to-constant mapping.** CS's ENB-compat runtime reproduces what ENB feeds `Params01`, and those indices match the tonemap shader's cbuffer registers c2 to c5. So `Param.x` = Receive Bloom Threshold, `Param.y` = **White x `fReinhardWhiteScale`**, `Cinematic.x/.z/.w` = saturation, contrast and brightness, `Tint` = rgb + amount, and `Fade` = rgb + amount scaled by `modAmount` ([CS ENBEffect.cpp](https://github.com/doodlum/skyrim-community-shaders/blob/dd2677fc4020db1da91b39cebef3dbfbe8913983/src/Features/Effects11/Effects/ENBEffect.cpp); [CS ISHDR.hlsl dev](https://github.com/doodlum/skyrim-community-shaders/blob/dd2677fc4020db1da91b39cebef3dbfbe8913983/package/Shaders/ISHDR.hlsl)). `Cinematic.y` is unused.

**[RECON] The HDR chain.** Luminance is downsampled through 16-tap and 4-tap passes (`DownSample16`, `DownSample4RGB2Lum`, `...LumClamp`) as a weighted **arithmetic** mean using Rec.709 weights (0.2125, 0.7154, 0.0721). **It is not a log-average.** Eye adaptation tracks two channels with a clamped exponential step that never overshoots, uses a minimum step of 1/256, and keeps the previous value on NaN ([CS ISHDR.hlsl @7e717921](https://github.com/doodlum/skyrim-community-shaders/blob/7e717921ee069572c2ec8b5ce93d403d2b5e5c76/package/Shaders/ISHDR.hlsl)):

```
delta     = new.xy - adapted.xy
adapted' = adapted + sign(delta) * clamp(abs(Param.wz * delta), 1/256, abs(delta))
```

Bloom is a bright-pass blur, `BlurBrightPass.y * max(0, sample - BlurBrightPass.x)`, in fixed 3 to 15 tap permutations (`ISBrightPassBlur3..15`) ([CS ISBlur.hlsl](https://github.com/doodlum/skyrim-community-shaders/blob/7e717921ee069572c2ec8b5ce93d403d2b5e5c76/package/Shaders/ISBlur.hlsl)). The tonemap, blend and cinematic step is identical in the 2023 and 2024 reconstructions ([CS ISHDR.hlsl @7e717921](https://github.com/doodlum/skyrim-community-shaders/blob/7e717921ee069572c2ec8b5ce93d403d2b5e5c76/package/Shaders/ISHDR.hlsl)):

```
L       = max(1e-5, luma(scene))
Lexp    = (avg.y / avg.x) * L
T       = Param.z > 0.5 ? HBD(Lexp) : Lexp * (1 + Param.y*Lexp) / (1 + Lexp)
blended = scene * (T / L) + saturate(Param.x - T) * bloom
Lb      = luma(blended)
graded  = lerp(avg.x,
               Cinematic.w * lerp(lerp(Lb, blended, Cinematic.x), Lb * Tint.rgb, Tint.w),
               Cinematic.z)
out     = pow(saturate(graded), 1/fGamma)
FADE:   out = lerp(out, Fade.rgb, Fade.w)
```

**[INFERENCE] Reading the math.** Exposure is `avg.y/avg.x`, so `avg.x` is adapted scene luminance and `avg.y` acts as the key. Eye Adapt Strength most plausibly controls how far the key follows the scene, but no source confirms how it reaches the shader. The tonemap scales RGB by a luminance ratio, so it is roughly hue-preserving. White is the `p` in `L(1+pL)/(1+L)`: at p = 1 the curve is the identity, and higher values compress highlights less. **Bloom is added only where the tonemapped luminance is below Receive Bloom Threshold**, so bloom brightens dark-to-mid pixels and 0 disables bloom reception entirely. **Tint multiplies luminance, not color**, so Tint.w = 1 yields a monochrome image in the tint color, matching the CK note that a full tint renders "in shades of that color". **Contrast pivots on adapted luminance** `avg.x`, not mid-grey. Saturation below 0 inverts hue, as the CK notes. The order is exposure, tonemap, bloom, saturation, tint, brightness, contrast, clamp, gamma, fade. The output is a plain `pow(x, 1/fGamma)` on a [0,1] value, where `fGamma` is the in-game Brightness slider (default 1.0) ([STEP SkyrimSE Display](https://stepmodifications.org/wiki/SkyrimSE:SkyrimPrefs_INI/Display)). **The game applies no sRGB OETF.** The constant 2.2 appears only inside the optional Hejl-Burgess-Dawson path, and no source identifies what toggles `Param.z` in vanilla. Sunlight Scale and Sky Scale are not read by any post shader. **[INFERENCE]** They multiply sun and sky brightness during scene rendering, which is why weather authors tune NAM0 and IMSP together.

**[CODE + DOC] IMADs.** An IMAD is a duration plus keyframed `NiFloatInterpolator` / `NiColorInterpolator` curves for mult/add HDR and cinematic terms, tint, fade, blur radius, double vision, radial blur, DoF and motion blur. Its "Target Lum Min/Max" curves are probably vestigial from Fallout 3/NV ([CommonLibSSE-NG TESImageSpaceModifier.h](https://github.com/CharmedBaryon/CommonLibSSE-NG/blob/main/include/RE/T/TESImageSpaceModifier.h)). Triggers include MGEF (Night Eye, vampire sight), EXPL, HAZD, CAMS camera shots (killmoves), any `TESImageSpaceModifiableForm`, weathers (`Sky::currentWeatherImageSpaceMod/Mod2`, `lastWeatherImageSpaceMod/Mod2`), and Papyrus `Apply`, `ApplyCrossFade`, `PopTo` and `Remove` ([Papyrus ImageSpaceModifier](https://skyrimck.uesp.net/wiki/ImageSpaceModifier_Script)). The CK documents the stacking rules: "most parameters use a multiply and add", **tint RGB is an alpha-weighted average with alpha = max**, and **blur radius and double vision take the max** across active modifiers ([CK Imagespace Modifier](https://ck.uesp.net/wiki/Imagespace_Modifier)). **[INFERENCE]** Per instance, the update is likely `v = v * lerp(1, mult(t), strength) + add(t) * strength`. How strength scales the terms across N instances is undocumented.

### How the final grade is composed at runtime

The runtime composition is the part most worth getting right in a reimplementation. Code confirms the structure, but the per-frame update function was not decompiled. **[CODE]** `ImageSpaceManager` (singleton `RELOCATION_ID(527731, 414660)`) holds `currentBaseData`, `overrideBaseData`, `underwaterBaseData` and `consoleBaseData`, plus `data`, which combines blended base data, 18 floats of mod data, `baseAmount` and `modAmount` ([CommonLibSSE-NG ImageSpaceManager.h](https://github.com/CharmedBaryon/CommonLibSSE-NG/blob/main/include/RE/I/ImageSpaceManager.h)). **[INFERENCE]** The likely composition runs in five steps:

1. Pick the base. Exteriors use the weather's four IMSP slots, interpolated by time of day, for both `lastWeather` and `currentWeather`, then lerped by `currentWeatherPct`. Interiors use the cell's XCIM or a room-bound INAM, and water has its own IMGS. Priority is plausibly console, then override, then underwater, then current, judged from member naming only.
2. Fold every active IMAD instance in with mult/add, alpha-weighted tint and max blur.
3. Multiply White by `fReinhardWhiteScale`.
4. Pack the result into the ISHDR cbuffer.
5. Apply `fGamma` last.

Sunlight Scale and Sky Scale branch off earlier, into scene lighting. Worldspaces have no IMGS of their own: the CK says image spaces "may not be applied to worldspaces directly", and `TESWorldSpace::kUseImageSpaceData` is marked unused ([CK ImageSpace](https://ck.uesp.net/wiki/ImageSpace)).

**[DOC] SE post additions and INI keys.** SE added TAA, SSR, SAO (with an optional compute variant), volumetric lighting (`bVolumetricLightingEnable`, `iVolumetricLightingQuality`), IBL lens flares (`bIBLFEnable`), improved snow and dynamic resolution. It also added `bUse64bitsHDRRenderTarget` (default 0), which "can significantly change the color of the image" ([STEP SkyrimSE Display](https://stepmodifications.org/wiki/SkyrimSE:SkyrimPrefs_INI/Display)). Whether that key switches the target from R11G11B10 to RGBA16F is unconfirmed. `iRadialBlurLevel` works only in Skyrim.ini, and `bDoDepthOfField` toggles DoF ([STEP Imagespace](https://stepmodifications.org/wiki/Guide:SkyrimPrefs_INI/Imagespace)). The DoF shader linearizes depth with separate planes for first person, can auto-focus on `AvgDepthTex`, and has "Fogged" and "Masked" variants ([CS ISDepthOfField.hlsl](https://github.com/doodlum/skyrim-community-shaders/blob/7e717921ee069572c2ec8b5ce93d403d2b5e5c76/package/Shaders/ISDepthOfField.hlsl)). **[INFERENCE]** LE lacks all the SE effects listed above. Its grading formula is believed to belong to the same family, because LE ENB also receives vanilla parameters, but no LE shader reconstruction exists to confirm it.

| 2. SKSE logic | Which engine lights exist and which get shadow slots | Light Placer, Intellightent, Shadow Boost, EVLaS, Disable Character Lighting | Calls and detours on `ShadowSceneNode`, globals, INI settings |

| 5. Final frame | LDR color only | ReShade | DXGI proxy on Present |

**Data mods** work entirely within vanilla limits, and many established practices exist only because of those limits. **[DOC]** These include mesh partitioning to dodge per-geometry caps ([COTN Morthal](https://www.nexusmods.com/skyrimspecialedition/mods/47245)), hidden "Light Occluder" meshes, "Just In Time" scripted light toggling, and region-linked emittance that tints window glows by time of day, all in Relighting Skyrim ([Relighting Skyrim SE](https://nexusmods.com/skyrimspecialedition/mods/8586)). They also include removing redundant shadow bulbs to stop popping. Weather mods rewrite NAM0, DALC, FNAM, IMSP and VOLI. NAT's "new tonemapping based on filmic curves" ([NAT](https://www.nexusmods.com/skyrimspecialedition/mods/12842)) must be **IMGS parameter tuning inside the fixed Reinhard shader** **[INFERENCE]**. Imaginator is an always-on scripted IMAD stacked on the weather's IMGS ([IMAGINATOR](https://www.nexusmods.com/skyrim/mods/13049)). The STEP guide warns that xEdit will not flag a conflict where one mod's lights are mixed with another mod's image spaces ([Kesta](https://stepmodifications.org/wiki/User:Kesta/LightingWeathersPostProcessing)).

**SKSE logic plugins** work through the engine's own light objects. **[CODE]** Light Placer creates an `NiPointLight` and fills `diffuse`, `radius.x/.y` and `radius.z = size`. It builds `LIGHT_CREATE_PARAMS` from reference flags (`neverFades`, `affectLand`, `affectWater`, `dynamic`) and calls `ShadowSceneNode::AddLight`. It hooks `TESObjectREFR::AddLight` (`RELOCATION_ID(19252, 19678)+0xB8`) along with effect, magic-caster and switch-node updates ([LightPlacer LightData.cpp](https://github.com/powerof3/LightPlacer/blob/master/src/LightData.cpp)). Its JSON schema exposes PortalStrict, Shadow, Simple and InverseSquare flags, plus ISL `cutoff` and `size` ([LightPlacer schema.json](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/powerof3/LightPlacer/master/schema.json)). These are real engine lights, so they are "subject to Skyrim's light limit" unless LLF is present ([Light Placer](https://www.nexusmods.com/skyrimspecialedition/mods/127557)). **[DOC]** Intellightent picks which 4 shadow casters stay active and demotes the rest to non-shadow lights ([STEP](https://stepmodifications.org/forum/topic/21445-intellightent-by-meh321/)). EVLaS took over the shadow incident vector before CS Sky Sync made it redundant.

**Community Shaders** replaces the shading layer itself. **[CODE]** Light Limit Fix hooks `SetupGeometry` (vtable index 6) on the Lighting, Effect and Water shaders. It patches the `ValidLight` call sites so that non-strict lights inside portal graphs drop out of vanilla per-object lists, keeping a light only if `portalStrict || !portalGraph || IsShadowLight()`. It then runs **clustered forward shading**: 64x64 px tiles x 32 log-depth slices (near 1, far 16384), `MAX_LIGHTS = 1024`, `CLUSTER_MAX_LIGHTS = 128`. Strict lights (portal-strict or shadowed, up to 15) go through `StrictLightDataCB` at b3 to preserve room masks (128-bit `roomFlags`) and shadow-mask semantics ([CS LightLimitFix.cpp](https://github.com/doodlum/skyrim-community-shaders/blob/dev/src/Features/LightLimitFix.cpp); [CS LightLimitFix.hlsli](https://github.com/doodlum/skyrim-community-shaders/blob/dev/features/Light%20Limit%20Fix/Shaders/LightLimitFix/LightLimitFix.hlsli)). DeepWiki's overview mentions a "16x16x32" grid, which conflicts with the 64 px tiles in source; trust the source. The pre-1.4 LLF page states that "the shadow limit is not yet unlocked" ([LLF](https://nexusmods.com/skyrimspecialedition/mods/99548)). Recent pre-releases add "up to 16 shadows", VRAM-aware shadow arrays and an Intellightent port ([CS v1.6.0-pr1941](https://newreleases.io/project/github/community-shaders/skyrim-community-shaders/release/v1.6.0-pr1941)), so the four-shadow cap is in the process of falling. Inverse Square Lighting computes `SCALED_UNITS_SQ/(d² + sizeBias) * smoothstep(saturate((radius - d)*fadeZone))` per flagged light ([CS ISL hlsli](https://github.com/doodlum/skyrim-community-shaders/blob/dd2677fc4020db1da91b39cebef3dbfbe8913983/features/Inverse%20Square%20Lighting/Shaders/InverseSquareLighting/InverseSquareLighting.hlsli)). IBL preserves vanilla DALC color ratios via `GetIBLRatio`, so weather mods keep their meaning ([DeepWiki IBL](https://deepwiki.com/doodlum/skyrim-community-shaders/4.4-image-based-lighting-and-skylighting)). On the grading side, the FAQ says "Vanilla HDR was replaced by a built-in tonemapper, and the related PP ini settings are no longer used" ([Open Shaders FAQ](https://github.com/alandtse/open-shaders/wiki/FAQ)). Current ISHDR keeps the vanilla structure but adds hue-preserving Reinhard with an HDR extension, a shadow-contrast fix, fade before gamma, and BT.2020/PQ output ([CS ISHDR.hlsl dev](https://github.com/doodlum/skyrim-community-shaders/blob/dd2677fc4020db1da91b39cebef3dbfbe8913983/package/Shaders/ISHDR.hlsl)). As of 1.4, CS dropped ENB-style particle lights in favour of Light Placer, and it disables itself if ENB is detected ([CS Nexus](https://www.nexusmods.com/skyrimspecialedition/mods/86492)).

**ENB** replaces the tonemap draw and ignores vanilla grading by default. **[DOC]** It is a `d3d11.dll` + `d3dcompiler_46e.dll` proxy ([STEP SkyrimSE:ENB](https://stepmodifications.org/wiki/SkyrimSE:ENB)). Vanilla IMGS and IMAD values arrive as `Params01[]` constants, and presets re-apply them only through "AGCC/AGIS" options, otherwise "These IS's get ignored by the ENB normally" ([Nexus article 832](https://www.nexusmods.com/skyrimspecialedition/articles/832)). ENB Image Space Fix exists to neutralise IMGS saturation, brightness, contrast and tint so that AGCC does not shift colors per location ([ENB Image Space Fix](https://www.nexusmods.com/skyrimspecialedition/mods/31475)). Preset authors detect Night Eye through `Params01[4].w` and `[5].w` ([Lucid ENB](https://www.nexusmods.com/skyrimspecialedition/mods/27481)). Per-weather tuning keys on weather FormIDs through ENB Helper and `_weatherlist.ini` ([STEP ENB Weather](https://stepmodifications.org/wiki/SkyrimSE:ENBSeries_INI_Reference/Weather)), which is why presets ship per weather mod. ENB complex particle lights sit **outside the engine's light list**: they are emitter-driven, invisible to NPCs, and not counted against the engine's light caps. That created the "ENB Light" mesh ecosystem ([ENB Light](https://www.nexusmods.com/skyrimspecialedition/mods/22574)). **[CODE]** CS's Effects 11 replicates the swap. It hooks `Main_HDRTonemapBlendCinematic_Render` (`RELOCATION_ID(99023, 105674)`) and skips the vanilla tonemap when `UseOriginalPostProcessing` is false ([CS Effects11.cpp](https://github.com/doodlum/skyrim-community-shaders/blob/dd2677fc4020db1da91b39cebef3dbfbe8913983/src/Features/Effects11.cpp)). That pins down the single draw where vanilla grading lives.

**ReShade** only sees the gamma-applied LDR backbuffer, plus depth if it is detected. It cannot change in-engine lighting ([STEP ReShade guide](https://stepmodifications.org/wiki/Guide:Reshade)).

## Conclusion

The picture that emerges is a renderer whose look sits almost entirely in **data and two small formulas**. The lighting formula is a gamma-space sum of DALC, emissive and up to 8 lights. The grading formula is a luminance-ratio Reinhard followed by four lerps. Neither is physically motivated, and both interact in ways that are easy to miss when reimplementing. Emissive and envmap are multiplied by diffuse irradiance. Bloom is gated by post-tonemap luminance. Contrast pivots on adapted luminance rather than mid-grey. Tint replaces chroma instead of multiplying it. A faithful Rust reimplementation can still discard most of Bethesda's machinery without visible change. CS shows that runtime branching can replace the permutation key, clustered forward shading can replace the 7-slot array, and per-object light lists only need to be preserved for portal-strict and shadowed lights. Three things do need to be preserved exactly: the gamma-space accumulation (CS's 1.6 exponent is the best available fit), DALC as an affine function of N, and the ISHDR operation order. Otherwise every existing weather, template and IMGS mod will look wrong.

The open questions are narrow and testable, which makes them good targets. The 4 versus 7 per-object conflict, the `Sky::UpdateColors` time-of-day curve, the IMAD strength-combination rule, how Eye Adapt Strength reaches `avg.y`, the HBD toggle, and whether vanilla uses sRGB SRVs could each be settled with a RenderDoc capture or a single decompiled function. None has been settled in public sources. LE remains essentially undocumented at the shader level, so any claim that LE "works the same way" is inference. Ecosystem momentum also matters for planning: through 2026, Community Shaders has absorbed Light Limit Fix, sun sync, Intellightent and ENB-preset loading, and has begun lifting the four-shadow cap. Those are the most constraining vanilla limits, so new work should assume CS plus Light Placer as the baseline rather than ENB particle lights or split meshes.
