short quotes; no code is copied. Licence caveat stated once here.

Headline: spud carries **authored weather data and authored material data all the way to the shader**, and
it implements the Skyrim lighting-shader families (rim/soft/back, Blinn-Phong gloss, model-space normals,
environment cubes, multilayer parallax, sparkle, projected MATO snow) on one vertex/fragment pair. Mudcrab
by contrast converts the same NIF material data but renders every surface through Bevy PBR and holds one
clear-day sky palette. The largest single visible gap on the Mudcrab side is **no sky model family response**
(no rim/soft/back, no environment cubes, no projected MATO snow), and the second is **no weather/time-of-day
state at all**. Two Mudcrab details matter for the comparison plan: its sun and ambient are a static
clear-weather pose (`crates/engine/src/app.rs:2364-2383`), and Bevy takes one ambient colour, not the six-face
`DALC` cube (`crates/engine/src/sky.rs:132-138`).

## Architecture

Two data paths meet in one frame: authored records become a small set of per-frame uniform blocks, and
authored NIF material data becomes a packed `GpuMaterial` row plus a technique/type word that the shader
switches on.

```
WTHR / CLMT / IMGS / IMAD / VOLI / SPGD / REGN  (plugins)
        │ atmosphere_import.cpp  (fix-ups, defaults, cloud layers, sky meshes)
        ▼
   .osa  ──> osa::Atmosphere ──> engine::Sky (weather.cpp)
                                   │  weights = f(hour, transition, 4 time keys)
                                   ├─> render::Environment  (directional light, DALC ambient, fog, IBL)
                                   └─> render::SkyFrame     (sky draws, image space, VL, precipitation, occlusion)
                                            │ renderer.cpp / renderer_post.cpp
                                            ▼
                        GpuFrame + GpuPost + GpuPrecip  ──> mesh.frag, sky_mesh.frag, precipitation.*, composite/post

NIF BSLightingShaderProperty / BSEffectShaderProperty / NiAlphaProperty
        │ mesh_import.cpp (params, flags, controllers)          │ world_import.cpp (STAT DNAM + MATO)
        ▼                                                       ▼
    .osm material params ──> meshes.cpp (technique, texture registers, specular/emit, MATO variants)
        │                                                       │
        └──────────> pipelines.cpp (Program x MeshVariant) <─────┘
                                   ▼
                        mesh.frag / effect.frag (single uber-shader)
```

Key ownership facts:

- One `Sky` object per session, "like the game's Sky singleton" ([`src/engine/weather.hpp:3-6`](https://github.com/chasmlol/spud/blob/8c9904207a5c573a0193fb903fb5c433f127f102/src/engine/weather.hpp#L3-L6)). It is
  updated once per frame from `App` before the environment is evaluated ([`src/engine/app.cpp:470-492`](https://github.com/chasmlol/spud/blob/8c9904207a5c573a0193fb903fb5c433f127f102/src/engine/app.cpp#L470-L492)).
- `SkyFrame` is plain values in fixed arrays so each `Environment` can carry one ([`src/render/sky.hpp:1-5`](https://github.com/chasmlol/spud/blob/8c9904207a5c573a0193fb903fb5c433f127f102/src/render/sky.hpp#L1-L5)).
  Draws are capped at 72 ([`src/render/sky.hpp:94`](https://github.com/chasmlol/spud/blob/8c9904207a5c573a0193fb903fb5c433f127f102/src/render/sky.hpp#L94)), precipitation at two emitters
  (`current`, `outgoing`; [`src/render/sky.hpp:102`](https://github.com/chasmlol/spud/blob/8c9904207a5c573a0193fb903fb5c433f127f102/src/render/sky.hpp#L102)).
- Weather selection is cell-driven: `enter_cell` picks the winning weather region from the `.osa` region
  polygons ([`src/engine/weather.cpp:549-577`](https://github.com/chasmlol/spud/blob/8c9904207a5c573a0193fb903fb5c433f127f102/src/engine/weather.cpp#L549-L577)), and `pick_for_region` / `choose_weather` apply the climate's
  weighted list ([`weather.cpp:579-645`](https://github.com/chasmlol/spud/blob/8c9904207a5c573a0193fb903fb5c433f127f102/src/engine/weather.cpp#L579-L645)).
- Lighting materials carry the technique in the high bits and feature bits below it
  ([`shaders/common.glsl:179-208`](https://github.com/chasmlol/spud/blob/8c9904207a5c573a0193fb903fb5c433f127f102/shaders/common.glsl#L179-L208), [`src/render/meshes.cpp:26-91`](https://github.com/chasmlol/spud/blob/8c9904207a5c573a0193fb903fb5c433f127f102/src/render/meshes.cpp#L26-L91)). Pipelines are specialised per material
  family so unused branches compile out ([`src/render/pipelines.hpp:106-127`](https://github.com/chasmlol/spud/blob/8c9904207a5c573a0193fb903fb5c433f127f102/src/render/pipelines.hpp#L106-L127), [`pipelines.cpp:261-295`](https://github.com/chasmlol/spud/blob/8c9904207a5c573a0193fb903fb5c433f127f102/src/render/pipelines.cpp#L261-L295)).
- All pipelines are built at startup, fanning out over the job system, with a persistent pipeline cache
  ([`src/render/pipelines.cpp:300-360`](https://github.com/chasmlol/spud/blob/8c9904207a5c573a0193fb903fb5c433f127f102/src/render/pipelines.cpp#L300-L360), [`:636-646`](https://github.com/chasmlol/spud/blob/8c9904207a5c573a0193fb903fb5c433f127f102/src/render/pipelines.cpp#L636-L646)). Nothing compiles at runtime.
- Precipitation occlusion is an offscreen depth view drawn before the scene, along the fall direction of
  the strongest emitter ([`src/engine/precip_occlusion.cpp:20-67`](https://github.com/chasmlol/spud/blob/8c9904207a5c573a0193fb903fb5c433f127f102/src/engine/precip_occlusion.cpp#L20-L67)), consumed by [`precipitation.frag:10-17`](https://github.com/chasmlol/spud/blob/8c9904207a5c573a0193fb903fb5c433f127f102/shaders/precipitation.frag#L10-L17).

## Mudcrab mapping

Mudcrab already converts most of the same authored material data. `docs/lighting-l1-compatibility.md`
states producer 27 publishes `shaderFamily`, `lightingShaderType`, `shaderFlags1/2` and `normalConvention`
on every material, and lists what is still missing. That table and this ledger agree on the families; the

## Same-pose comparison plan

Goal: separate *material* differences from *weather* differences. Do not compare two scenes that differ in
both. Every step is offline and headless.

Fixtures: use the same converted assets on both engines. spud reads `.osm`/`.osa` from its own cache; Mudcrab
reads its GLB/KTX2 and the SQLite catalog, so the first gate is that the same NIF and the same WTHR FormID
produce the same authored numbers (compare dumped bytes, not pixels).

Poses (Creation units, same `hfov` and frame size; Mudcrab `--shots` JSON, spud `camera free`):

| Pose | Where | What it isolates |
|---|---|---|
| P1 | Riverwood overlook, clear weather `0x81A`, hour 12 | fur/wood/cloth rim and soft light, specular on metal |
| P2 | Alpine slope with `MountainTrimSlab_LightSN` (`0x6F275`) in view, hour 12 | MATO single-pass snow coverage and falloff |
| P3 | Same slope, night hour 1, stars and moons visible | sky background, moon phase, star alpha |
| P4 | Under a roof edge in rain, `SkyrimStormRain`-class weather, hour 12 | precipitation occlusion line and rain slant |
| P5 | Interior cell with XCLL and one torch (`Bleak Falls` first hall) | interior lighting and point-light falloff |

Procedure per pose:

1. Fix the state: spud `set weather <FormID>`, `set time_of_day <h>`, `set animation_time <t>`,
   `set fog_off 0`, `set hold_actors 1`, `wait_loaded` ([`docs/AUTOMATION.md:23`](https://github.com/chasmlol/spud/blob/8c9904207a5c573a0193fb903fb5c433f127f102/docs/AUTOMATION.md#L23), [`:32-33`](https://github.com/chasmlol/spud/blob/8c9904207a5c573a0193fb903fb5c433f127f102/docs/AUTOMATION.md#L32-L33)); Mudcrab the same
   weather FormID and hour through its fixture/config, `--shots` with the identical pose, and the default
   `vivid` preset, which `crates/engine/src/color_pipeline.rs:417-444` defines as the approved clear-day
   grade; record the preset in the receipt so a `cool-gray` run cannot be mistaken for it.
2. Prove the material family: spud `set debug_view technique` and `material`, Mudcrab's material/probe view.
   Assert the same family at the same pixels before comparing colour.
3. Capture: spud `screenshot_hdr out.npy` and `screenshot out.png`; Mudcrab's HDR readback (`hdr_probe.rs`,
   `capture_frame.rs`). Store both with the pose, weather FormID, hour, MSAA count and binary checksums.
4. Attribute: compare channel-wise in masked regions (sky band, rock, fur, metal). A difference that appears
   in the albedo/debug views is a conversion problem; one that appears only in the lit view is a lighting
   problem; one that moves with the hour is weather.
5. Isolate single terms: spud `set sun_shadow 1` (everything in shadow, direct off) leaves rim/soft/back
   visible ([`docs/AUTOMATION.md:32`](https://github.com/chasmlol/spud/blob/8c9904207a5c573a0193fb903fb5c433f127f102/docs/AUTOMATION.md#L32)); `set draw_blended 0` removes blended draws; `screenshot_shadow` gives
   the cascade data. Use these to test one term at a time instead of guessing from the final image.
6. Repeat at hour 6, 12, 18 and 0 for P1 only, once per weather. This is the gate for #364/#546 and settles
   the "raw authored domain" question only if both engines are driven from the same record bytes.

Acceptance for the material slice (ranks 1-3): for P1 and P2, the debug views agree and the lit HDR differs
by less than a stated per-region threshold, with the residual listed by cause. Acceptance for weather
(rank 4): the same at four hours and two weathers, plus a transition capture (T = 0.5) at a fixed hour.
Do not run a full conversion or launch either game ; these are plans only.

## Port notes

- Build the technique word once, at conversion or load, from `flags1/flags2` in the same order as
  [`meshes.cpp:26-91`](https://github.com/chasmlol/spud/blob/8c9904207a5c573a0193fb903fb5c433f127f102/src/render/meshes.cpp#L26-L91). The order matters: later type rules override earlier ones, and types 7 and 18 are
  remapped. Re-deriving it in WGSL will drift.
- Do not port `amb_spec` or the IBL term: spud fills both and no shader reads `amb_spec`, while `ibl.x` is 0.
  They are dead weight, not missing features.
- The specular path is Blinn-Phong with the authored gloss as the exponent, then Toksvig widening from the
  mip-filtered normal ([`mesh.frag:56-59`](https://github.com/chasmlol/spud/blob/8c9904207a5c573a0193fb903fb5c433f127f102/shaders/mesh.frag#L56-L59), [`:150-158`](https://github.com/chasmlol/spud/blob/8c9904207a5c573a0193fb903fb5c433f127f102/shaders/mesh.frag#L150-L158)). Bevy's PBR path uses `reflectance`/F0; a Skyrim-profile
  branch must replace, not add to, Bevy's specular or highlights double.
- The rim/soft/back terms are added with the *unshadowed* light colour and are not scaled by the shadow mask
  ([`mesh.frag:39-55`](https://github.com/chasmlol/spud/blob/8c9904207a5c573a0193fb903fb5c433f127f102/shaders/mesh.frag#L39-L55), [`:220-222`](https://github.com/chasmlol/spud/blob/8c9904207a5c573a0193fb903fb5c433f127f102/shaders/mesh.frag#L220-L222)). That is the single most visible rule in `add_light`.
- MATO needs per-reference material variants. spud caches them per (mesh, base FormID) and per
  (mesh, base FormID, multi-pass) ([`meshes.cpp:948-960`](https://github.com/chasmlol/spud/blob/8c9904207a5c573a0193fb903fb5c433f127f102/src/render/meshes.cpp#L948-L960), [`:1081-1086`](https://github.com/chasmlol/spud/blob/8c9904207a5c573a0193fb903fb5c433f127f102/src/render/meshes.cpp#L1081-L1086)). In Bevy, key material instances the same
  way, cap the number per mesh, and free them with the reference, or the material arena grows with cells.
- The single-pass MATO path ignores the MATO's projection vector and uses the fixed top-down basis
  ([`meshes.cpp:527`](https://github.com/chasmlol/spud/blob/8c9904207a5c573a0193fb903fb5c433f127f102/src/render/meshes.cpp#L527), [`mesh.vert:87-90`](https://github.com/chasmlol/spud/blob/8c9904207a5c573a0193fb903fb5c433f127f102/shaders/mesh.vert#L87-L90)). Spud's comment claims this is the game's behaviour; it is
  `unverified` against retail. Verify before copying the choice.
- Weather blending is linear over four (weather, time) weights and clamps to 1 before lighting
  ([`weather.cpp:1037-1053`](https://github.com/chasmlol/spud/blob/8c9904207a5c573a0193fb903fb5c433f127f102/src/engine/weather.cpp#L1037-L1053)). Whether Skyrim blends in the authored byte domain is Mudcrab open gate 3
  (#364) and is not settled by spud.
- Fog is linear in Skyrim's clip-space length ([`mesh.vert:104-108`](https://github.com/chasmlol/spud/blob/8c9904207a5c573a0193fb903fb5c433f127f102/shaders/mesh.vert#L104-L108)) with day/night authored ends; Mudcrab's
  Bevy `DistanceFog` is visibility-based (`sky.rs:292-334`). Matching colours is not enough; the curves
  differ. Port the formula or accept a listed residual.
- Cloud and sky draws are ordered: masks before stars, stars before discs, clouds last, each with a fixed
  depth bias of 3 or 9 units of `2^-24` ([`weather.cpp:19-20`](https://github.com/chasmlol/spud/blob/8c9904207a5c573a0193fb903fb5c433f127f102/src/engine/weather.cpp#L19-L20), [`:1244-1487`](https://github.com/chasmlol/spud/blob/8c9904207a5c573a0193fb903fb5c433f127f102/src/engine/weather.cpp#L1244-L1487)). Order and bias are load-bearing.
- Precipitation hashes its particles from a seed ([`precipitation.vert:11-22`](https://github.com/chasmlol/spud/blob/8c9904207a5c573a0193fb903fb5c433f127f102/shaders/precipitation.vert#L11-L22)) so a pose repeats. Any port
  that uses a time-seeded stream loses the comparison.
- The occlusion pass draws only land and opaque objects: water and blended draws are off
  ([`precip_occlusion.cpp:58-59`](https://github.com/chasmlol/spud/blob/8c9904207a5c573a0193fb903fb5c433f127f102/src/engine/precip_occlusion.cpp#L58-L59)). Rain still falls through a glass roof in spud; a port should decide this
  explicitly.
- The strongest emitter alone places the occlusion map ([`precip_occlusion.cpp:23-31`](https://github.com/chasmlol/spud/blob/8c9904207a5c573a0193fb903fb5c433f127f102/src/engine/precip_occlusion.cpp#L23-L31)); a script emitter
  overlapping weather rain uses the larger amount.

Minimal vertical slice that proves the subsystem: one exterior cell, one weather FormID, one hour, one
camera pose, one mesh per material family (default with rim/soft, env-map, MSN) spawned with `spawn`
([`docs/AUTOMATION.md:29`](https://github.com/chasmlol/spud/blob/8c9904207a5c573a0193fb903fb5c433f127f102/docs/AUTOMATION.md#L29)), and a check that switches `debug_view material` and `technique` on the same pose.
The exact check: the family word and technique at three known pixels match between engines, then the lit
HDR differs only in the regions the family touches.
