Ranked by player-visible effect, the three findings that matter most:

1. **Grass in spud is generated at runtime from LAND + LTEX, bit-exactly like the game; Mudcrab bakes placement at conversion (PR #282) and renders it (#539).** Both are viable; spud's version needs no new cache and no 7,173-cell bake, but it moves the cost to the frame's background threads. Mudcrab already enforces its own per-cell ceilings of 10/50/100 ms at spacings 64/32/20 (PR #540), which is the same cost class; spud's per-cell time is unmeasured here. The choice affects E6/E7, not the look.
2. **Octahedral tree impostors (8x8 hemi-octahedral atlas, coverage in alpha, per-frame 3-frame blend, coverage-preserving alpha) are the single biggest far-view visual win available.** Mudcrab has billboards only (`docs/specs/converters/billboard-generation.md:40-43`), and spud replaces Skyrim's flat billboards on purpose ([`docs/ARCHITECTURE.md:116`](https://github.com/chasmlol/spud/blob/8c9904207a5c573a0193fb903fb5c433f127f102/docs/ARCHITECTURE.md#L116)).
3. **Water: spud's expensive parts (stencil mask, reflection cube, SSR mix, water history) are not the visual gap; the visible gap is depth-graded shallow/deep colour, depth fog and refraction, which #299 already targets.** Spud's flow map and the "reject refraction samples above the plane" rule are the two cheap, portable extras.

**Milestone fit.** E5 (DDS/KTX2, `docs/roadmap/02-visual-fidelity-implementation-plan.md:378`) is where a spud-style port pays first: an impostor atlas adds a new texture kind (BC3 with coverage in alpha, KTX2 for Mudcrab) and a macro land map adds BC1/BC5 map kinds, both with coverage-weighted mips. E6 (strict runtime, same file line 379) then has to bind those kinds, fall back when absent, and keep the strict-mode evidence honest; the silent defaults listed below are exactly the cases E6 must convert into loud failures.

## Visual-risk checklist (ranked by player-visible effect)

For each item: what a player sees if Mudcrab adopts it, and what can go wrong.

1. **Octahedral tree impostors.** Effect: trees keep volume and correct lighting at 300-3000 m instead of flat cards; the swap to full trees becomes hard to see. Risks: (a) atlas seams if dilation is missing ([`far_trees.cpp:268-269`](https://github.com/chasmlol/spud/blob/8c9904207a5c573a0193fb903fb5c433f127f102/src/importer/far/far_trees.cpp#L268-L269)); (b) dark, dense crowns if coverage is not preserved through mips ([`impostor.frag:51-58`](https://github.com/chasmlol/spud/blob/8c9904207a5c573a0193fb903fb5c433f127f102/shaders/impostor.frag#L51-L58)); (c) double trees at the swap — spud deliberately overlaps by 1 m ([`impostor.vert:36-42`](https://github.com/chasmlol/spud/blob/8c9904207a5c573a0193fb903fb5c433f127f102/shaders/impostor.vert#L36-L42)); (d) alpha-test threshold mismatch with Mudcrab's 128/255 default (`billboard-generation.md:29-31`); (e) batch cost: 3 frame taps + 3 normal taps per pixel ([`impostor.frag:36-66`](https://github.com/chasmlol/spud/blob/8c9904207a5c573a0193fb903fb5c433f127f102/shaders/impostor.frag#L36-L66)) — spud drops to one tap under 24 px ([`impostor.vert:77-84`](https://github.com/chasmlol/spud/blob/8c9904207a5c573a0193fb903fb5c433f127f102/shaders/impostor.vert#L77-L84)).
2. **Grass parity details.** Effect: bare or thinned grassland where the LAND layer stack is deep ([`grass.cpp:210`](https://github.com/chasmlol/spud/blob/8c9904207a5c573a0193fb903fb5c433f127f102/src/engine/grass.cpp#L210), 7th layer ignored) or where species exceed the 3-per-texture cap. Mudcrab's bake already handles the 7th layer (PR #282). Risk if spud's rules are copied: losing the 208 quadrants #282 fixed.
3. **Water depth colour and fog.** Effect: rivers and lakes stop looking like a flat tinted sheet; shallow edges show the bed. Risks: the depth fog power `c8.w` can silently disable the whole mix at 0 ([`water.frag:169`](https://github.com/chasmlol/spud/blob/8c9904207a5c573a0193fb903fb5c433f127f102/shaders/water.frag#L169)); refraction without plane rejection shows the sky through a shoreline ([`water.frag:164-166`](https://github.com/chasmlol/spud/blob/8c9904207a5c573a0193fb903fb5c433f127f102/shaders/water.frag#L164-L166)); screen-space refraction smears at the screen edge (clamped UVs, [`water.frag:161-162`](https://github.com/chasmlol/spud/blob/8c9904207a5c573a0193fb903fb5c433f127f102/shaders/water.frag#L161-L162)).
4. **Grass draw order and fade.** Effect: popping when cells stream in. Spud fades per cell over `fGrassFadeInTime` but caps the gain at 0.1/frame ([`grass.cpp:531`](https://github.com/chasmlol/spud/blob/8c9904207a5c573a0193fb903fb5c433f127f102/src/engine/grass.cpp#L531)), so the fade length depends on frame rate; and eviction is silent ([`:460-462`](https://github.com/chasmlol/spud/blob/8c9904207a5c573a0193fb903fb5c433f127f102/src/engine/grass.cpp#L460-L462)). Risk if copied: grass that never appears at long view distances, or a visible ring of cells fading in/out at the cull radius.
5. **Macro land map.** Effect: distant terrain keeps its colour and slope shading at a fraction of the geometry cost. Risks: `k_map_gain` is shader-specific ([`far_land.cpp:31-33`](https://github.com/chasmlol/spud/blob/8c9904207a5c573a0193fb903fb5c433f127f102/src/importer/far/far_land.cpp#L31-L33)); BC5 normal Z reconstruction must match; a black default texture silently darkens a whole worldspace ([`:170-173`](https://github.com/chasmlol/spud/blob/8c9904207a5c573a0193fb903fb5c433f127f102/src/importer/far/far_land.cpp#L170-L173)).
6. **HLOD proxies.** Effect: fewer draw calls and steadier frame time in settlements. Risks: material identity drift (the byte-wise key, [`far_proxies.cpp:129-133`](https://github.com/chasmlol/spud/blob/8c9904207a5c573a0193fb903fb5c433f127f102/src/importer/far/far_proxies.cpp#L129-L133)), over-aggressive simplification at 0.3 m, and the stripping of features the far shader still needs ([`:46-56`](https://github.com/chasmlol/spud/blob/8c9904207a5c573a0193fb903fb5c433f127f102/src/importer/far/far_proxies.cpp#L46-L56)).
7. **Water cube/history.** Effect: none that a player would call an improvement over Mudcrab's planar reflection; both add artifacts (axis-aligned cube, ghosting). Do not port.
8. **Flow atlas.** Effect: less flow-texture popping on rivers at cell borders; negligible unless flow maps are already visible. Low risk, low value.
